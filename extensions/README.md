hold a step, press track 1–8. every note on that step gets that velocity:

| track | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 | 7 | 8 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| velocity | 4 | 8 | 16 | 32 | 64 | 100 | 112 | 127 |

the step's velocity lights up pink on its track button. track buttons don't switch tracks while a step is held.

hold an empty step + track 1 and 8: all leds pink, same again to turn off.
